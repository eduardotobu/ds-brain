I dont only want my classifier to tell me to sell or buy, i also want to say how much do i expect the cost will increase, the extent of the increase.

Lots of problems in daily life are ideally regression problems where we care about the continuous value.

The setup of the problem is very similar as those for classification: We would be provided n examples (feature vectors) and instead of labels, in this case we are getting actual values. 
The key difference here is that instead of assuming that our $y$ comes from -1 or +1, we will assume that our $y$ comes from continuos values, they come from $\mathbb{R}$. 

**The goal of our model will be to learn how to map feature vectors into continuous values.**
